ANALYTICS & RESEARCH

Hazen researchers seek to understand the behavior and needs of our players as they use our apps, websites and games.

ART, ANIMATION & SOUND

Artists work with a variety of tools to make fantasies real and craft inviting worlds that fuel the creativity of your peers.

CORPORATE OPERATIONS

Operations handles consumer products, human resources, legal, finance, and even more work related to our games in-house to make sure everything we do is polished until it shines.

GAME DESIGN

ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY

Recruitment Process

01

Application

If you find an appealing job opportunity on our careers site, don't hesitate to submit your application. We actively engage with potentially suiting candidates and the positions are filled as soon as we find the right person for the job.

Applying should be quick and straightforward: upload your resume and - if required - remember to attach your portfolio or reel.

02

FIRST INTERVIEW

If you find an appealing job opportunity on our careers site, don't hesitate to submit your application. We actively engage with potentially suiting candidates and the positions are filled as soon as we find the right person for the job.If you're located outside the Greater Helsinki or Stockholm area, no problem! We will begin the discussion with you via Zoom.

First interview's objective is to learn more about you, introduce ourselves, and tell you more about the job and the studio.

03

COMPETENCE-BASED ASSESSMENT

The next step is to evaluate your competencies and experience. For most of our roles, we have a practical assessment that will be reviewed by members of our teams. If the feedback is positive, we proceed to a competence- focused interview with people from the relevant craft.

04

FINAL INTERVIEWS

The last step of the recruitment process is meant for you to meet the team, learn more about our projects, and get a better understanding of our studio culture, which often takes a full day.

If you are already located close to one of our offices or if the circumstances allow, we would like to organize this step as an in-person visit to our studio. Otherwise, we will arrange a remote alternative.

05

OFFER

If the feeling of a match is still mutual, we will make you a job offer. When the offer has been signed, we can start planning your on-boarding together.
