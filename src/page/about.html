ABOUT HAZEN

Established in 2017, HAZEN Games is a global video game company dedicated to gaming development, publication, and esports. With more than 1,600 employees worldwide, the company operates offices in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines, Latin America, Korea, and China. We have successfully launched several high-profile games globally and has built long-term relationships with governments and esports organizations in more than 30 countries worldwide.Human Vanguard is wining Game of the Year 2022, available on the PC, Xbox & PlayStation, it has over 1.5 billion downloaded and a monthly active user base of over 110 million players, as well as being among the top 10 most-played games in over 80 countries.

Our Journey

First Game

In November 2016, Void Templar was launched worldwide and was listed on PC, Xbox, and PlayStation. In 2017, it won the Best Visual Art Award. Even though it was made by a team of less than 70 people, the game became a big success. This was a great start for Hazen Company to grow and create more games.

Tournament

In May 2020, HAZEN held first World wide Exo Clash  offline esports tournament in Korea. The enthusiasm of the gamers was very good, the total number of offline viewers was one million viewers and the online viewers on YouTube reached five million viewers, this made the demand for the next tournaments even greater.

Games

In 2024, Hazen had created a total of six games across various genres and platforms, including PC, Xbox, PlayStation, and cross-platform. Globally, they had been downloaded over 1.5 billion times and had a monthly active user base of over 110 million players.

Achievement

By 2025, Hazen had received several awards, including Aeterna Chronicles Game of the Year 2024, Blade & Souls Steam Best Action Game 2023, Human Vanguard Game of the Year 2022 (Fan Choice), Exo Clash Best Multiplayer Game 2021, Iron Legion Best Innovation & Visual Arts Game 2019, and Void Templar Best Visual Arts Award 2017.
